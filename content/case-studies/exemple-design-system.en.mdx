## Context

At a logistics and last-mile orchestration SaaS scale-up, multiple product squads ship across heterogeneous front-end stacks. The Design team needed to support high delivery velocity while protecting bandwidth for upstream Product Discovery.

## The problem

Unchecked AI UI generation quickly leads to *skip-discovery* and interface debt: reinvented components, broken accessibility rules, and heavy back-and-forth during design reviews.

## Approach

1. **Discovery first**: enforced methodological guardrails (usage analytics, personas, business goals) before any UI generation or prototyping begins.
2. **Unified source of truth**: tight alignment across Figma libraries, design tokens, and component documentation.
3. **MCP server & deterministic checks**: exposed the Design System and composition rules through an MCP server so AI agents used by PMs and engineers assemble compliant screens on the first pass.

## Outcomes & learnings

- PMs and engineers ship standard flows autonomously with high design compliance
- Significantly less friction between Design and Engineering
- Design team bandwidth refocused on deep user research and complex workflows
