## Context

Logistics orchestration tools are operated under tight time constraints by diverse users, many of whom are not technical specialists. When an integration or carrier error happens, the product must avoid feeling like an opaque tool built only for insiders.

## Approach

1. **Usage data & support analysis**: pinpointed recurring friction points and support escalations caused by configuration or carrier errors.
2. **Cognitive reassurance**: redesigned error states and recovery flows to clearly state what happened, what data is safe, and the exact next step to resolve the issue.
3. **Testing & iteration**: validated with operations teams against real incident scenarios.

## Outcomes

- Reduced operator stress during critical incidents
- Higher self-service resolution rate without escalating to technical support
